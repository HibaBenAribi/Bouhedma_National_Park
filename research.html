Research

Multiple research studies are performed in the Bouhedma National Park. Park conservators and visiting scientists work together here throughout the year — on species inventories, long-term monitoring plots, camera traps, ultrasonic detectors, acoustic recorders, vegetation transects and studies of wildlife–human coexistence.

Learning resources

In the summer of 2026, WWF North Africa organised the camp “Human Wildlife Coexistence for Conservation” in Bouhedma National Park. Participants from diverse backgrounds were trained by field experts in modern conservation, reintroduction and safe human-wildlife coexistence.
